Who We Are

A Community Ecosystem
Built from the Ground Up

Pleasant Beginnings Inc. is a Baltimore-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded in 2020 by Taneisha "TeeLee" Lee. What began as a vision to reduce youth violence through entrepreneurship has grown into one of Baltimore's most comprehensive community ecosystems — spanning seven active youth programs, family services, behavioral health partnerships, creative economy platforms, workforce development, and global media.

We serve youth from elementary through high school — and we serve their families too. Because we know the truth: a youth cannot thrive when their home is in crisis. So we work with the whole family unit, providing the stability behind the student.

Pleasant Beginnings operates across Baltimore City, Baltimore County, and surrounding counties — with programs, partnerships, and platforms that reach nationally and globally through 4CornersTV and the Stitch by Stitch Creative Network.

We don't just run programs. We build the infrastructure that makes lasting change possible.

Our Mission

To break cycles of violence in Baltimore and beyond by equipping youth, families, and communities with the tools, opportunities, and platforms they need to build sustainable futures — through entrepreneurship, creative expression, education, wellness, and wraparound support.

Our Vision

A safer, stronger Baltimore — and a model for cities nationwide — where every young person has access to entrepreneurship, creative opportunity, and community support; where families are stable and resourced; and where the cycle of violence is replaced by a cycle of generational wealth, leadership, and purpose.

The Six Divisions

I
Youth Empowerment
& Violence Prevention

Building Baltimore's Next Generation of Leaders

Seven active, curriculum-driven programs bring entrepreneurship, media, fitness, music therapy, leadership, sports business, and community relations directly into schools and after-school settings across Baltimore City & County. Every curriculum was authored by TeeLee Lee.

In-SchoolAfter-SchoolAssemblyB2G Purchasing
II
Family Stability
& Community Services

Stabilizing the Foundation Behind Every Young Person

PB's Job Finder Program has placed clients within weeks of enrollment. Our resource brokering — powered by the Johns Hopkins Medi System with 3,000+ resources — connects families to financial literacy, credit repair, housing navigation, eviction support, and more. The E.R.A. Empower Rental Alliance builds healthy landlord-tenant relationships. The Adopt-a-Lot initiative beautifies and reclaims community spaces.

Job PlacementFinancial LiteracyHousingResource Brokering
III
Health, Wellness
& Behavioral Support

Healing the Whole Person

PB's PRP Support & Engagement Platform — in partnership with Redesigning Minds — connects individuals to psychiatric rehabilitation services through a three-tier model (Referral Only, Full Partnership, Programming Access), generating 15+ qualified referrals per month with a 75%+ 60-day retention rate. The EmpowerWell senior wellness program ensures no generation is left behind. PB also serves as a wraparound support partner for NexTech Academy's tech training students.

PRP PlatformMental HealthSenior WellnessWraparound Support
IV
Creative Economy
& Cultural Impact

The Bridge Between Artists, Nonprofits & Community

The Stitch by Stitch (SBS) Creative Network is the first nonprofit-powered platform connecting struggling artists, musicians, influencers, and creatives directly to nonprofit organizations — so both grow together. Artists earn real income. Nonprofits gain cultural reach and authentic storytelling. Fashion Meets Philanthropy (with Bombcity) fuses fashion and fundraising into quarterly runway events. PBTV — PB's dedicated channel on the global 4CornersTV streaming platform — distributes original programming worldwide, including the talk show Tea & Truth with BJ Dawkins.

SBS NetworkFashion Meets PhilanthropyPBTV · 4CornersTVTea & Truth
V
Curriculum &
Program Development

Building the Programs Behind the Programs

Every one of PB's seven youth programs was built from the ground up — TeeLee Lee wrote each curriculum from the vision, passion, and expertise of each program lead. This capability is now available as a service for nonprofits, entrepreneurs, and organizations ready to build and launch their own programming. From concept to curriculum to delivery framework — PB makes your idea fundable, scalable, and real.

Curriculum WritingProgram DesignNonprofit ConsultingLaunch Support
VI
Workforce Development
& Tech Pathways

From the Community Into the Economy

Through a formal MOU with NexTech Academy, PB provides comprehensive wraparound support — case management, mental health referrals, career readiness, financial literacy, and basic needs — that keeps young adults in tech certification programs through completion. A state apprenticeship approval is currently in progress, which will unlock workforce funding pathways for both organizations. PB's Job Finder Program has a documented track record of client placement within weeks.
